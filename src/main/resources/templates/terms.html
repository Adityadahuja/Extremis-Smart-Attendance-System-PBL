Extremis Terms & Conditions

Welcome to Extremis, the role-based student attendance system. By using this application, you agree to the following terms regarding privacy, permissions, and attendance integrity.

1. Purpose of Location Verification

Extremis captures high-accuracy GPS coordinates strictly at the moment you initiate a Check In or Check Out event. Your coordinates are verified against the authorized campus geofence (such as Arya College of Engineering & IT, Kukas, Jaipur). We do NOT perform continuous background location tracking.

2. Live Camera Capture

To prevent proxy attendance, check-in requires a real-time photo taken directly via your device's camera. Image uploads from gallery or local files are blocked. Photos are stored securely on the institutional server and are accessible only to authorized faculty and administrators.

3. Single Active Device Policy

For security and verification integrity, a student account is permitted to be logged in on only ONE device at any time. Attempting to log in on a secondary device while a session is active will be blocked.

4. Institutional Compliance

Attendance records generated through Extremis are official institutional records and subject to university attendance regulations.
